$ Git Game: theme directions

Five directions. Each is drawn in dark and light, and each panel checks its own 48 colour pairs (WCAG AA, the same set as MASTER.md §4) and shows how many pass. All five keep the deck's file colours, the semantic colours' meanings (red is a rejection or a bug, green is accepted, amber is a warning, pink is you), and an always-dark terminal. Palette and shape are independent, so you can mix them, e.g. the Terminal palette with Tabletop's outlined cards.

  • Commit violet

Terminal (the proposal)

Today's look, fixed: near-black and warm paper, logo teal, mono headings.

  • Accent: logo teal #3EC1B3 (dark), #13756B (light). Ground: today's near-black and warm paper, nudged for contrast.
  • Shape: 14 px surfaces, 10 px controls, 1 px lines, flat. Type: mono headings with $ and #, sans prose.
  • Trade-off: the safest choice, and the least new.

Commit violet

The logo's other colour leads: the violet commit dot becomes the accent, on an ink-blue night.

  • Accent: the logo's commit violet, #B39DFB at night and #5B3CC4 by day. The ground is an ink blue, not grey.
  • Shape: a little rounder (16 / 12 px), with a soft violet glow under the cards.
  • Trade-off: violet is also the api.py card colour, so a primary button can rhyme with that card. Teal stays in the logo and the $ prompt.

Tabletop

The print-and-play deck on a table: cream card stock and ink outlines by day, green felt by night.

  • Accent: ink itself. The primary button is black on cream by day and cream on felt by night; teal becomes the link colour.
  • Shape: 6 px corners, 2 px ink outlines and a hard 3 px offset shadow on cards, like card stock on a table. It echoes the print-and-play deck.
  • Trade-off: heavier lines add visual weight to the busy hub, and the dark felt is the furthest from today's look.

Phosphor / Printout

All terminal, no chrome: true black and a brighter teal at night, a plain printout by day. Mono everywhere and square corners. It could double as a high-contrast mode.

  • Accent: a brighter teal #4FF0DA on true black; a deep teal #00574F on white paper.
  • Shape: square corners, no shadows, 1 px lines. Type: mono for everything, prose included.
  • All of its own text is 7:1 or more (AAA); only the deck's fixed band colours sit at 6.2–8.8:1.
  • Trade-off: mono prose is slower to read on long pages (/privacy, /teach), so it may suit an optional high-contrast mode better than the default.

Studio

Friendlier for people new to Git and for classrooms: cool slate, rounder shapes, sans headings, and the $ prompt kept only where Git speaks.

  • Accent: a softer teal #5CD6C8 at night, #0F7168 by day, on cool slate instead of warm paper.
  • Shape: 20 px surfaces and pill buttons. Type: sans headings without the $ and # prompts; mono only where Git speaks.
  • Trade-off: friendlier for /new-to-git and classrooms, but it gives up the terminal headings that make Git Game recognisable.
